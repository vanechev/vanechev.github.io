Research Projects

My work spans four interconnected research areas, all aimed at making AI-powered analytics more human-centred, interpretable, and actionable in high-stakes settings. Select a card to read more about each area's goals, methods, and key publications.

Teamwork Analytics

Designing AI-powered analytics that help healthcare teams and educators make sense of complex multimodal data captured during simulations — supporting reflection, debriefing, and skill development in high-stakes, team-based settings.

Data Storytelling & Interpretability
