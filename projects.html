Research Projects

My work spans four interconnected research streams, all aimed at making AI-powered analytics more human-centred, interpretable, and actionable in high-stakes settings.

Multimodal & Embodied Learning Analytics

Foundations for capturing and modelling learning in physical and embodied settings. I develop frameworks and tools to collect and interpret audio, video, physiological, spatial, and interaction data from co-located learning environments.

Key contributions include the HuCETA framework for human-centred embodied teamwork analytics and the Moodoo indoor positioning system for characterising classroom teaching.
