Embodied Interaction

📡

Figure placeholder — a classroom floor plan overlaid with movement traces from the Moodoo indoor positioning system, alongside synchronised audio and physiological streams used by the HuCETA framework to characterise embodied teamwork.

This research area lays the foundations for capturing and modelling learning as it actually happens — in physical, co-located spaces, where people move, talk, gesture, and collaborate. I develop frameworks and tools to collect and interpret multimodal data, including audio, video, physiological signals, spatial positioning, and interaction logs, from embodied learning environments such as classrooms and clinical simulation rooms.

Two contributions anchor this stream: the HuCETA framework, a human-centred approach to embodied teamwork analytics, and Moodoo, an indoor positioning system that characterises teachers' movement and pedagogical approaches in physical classrooms.
