About

I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in the School of Computing Technologies at RMIT University 🇦🇺, and a Research Affiliate at Monash University 🇦🇺 and Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL) 🇪🇨.

My research lies at the intersection of human-centred artificial intelligence, learning analytics, and simulation-based education, with a focus on healthcare and other high-stakes professional domains. My work investigates how multimodal data — including audio, video, physiological, spatial, and interaction data — can be transformed into interpretable, trustworthy, and actionable analytics to support learning, reflection, and decision-making.

Prior to joining RMIT, I held postdoctoral research fellow positions at Monash University (2022–2025) and Carnegie Mellon University (HCII) 🇺🇸 (2020). I completed my PhD in Learning Analytics at the University of Technology Sydney (2020) 🇦🇺 and my MSc. in Artificial Intelligence Cum Laude at KU Leuven (2013) 🇧🇪.
